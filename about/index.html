Background & approach

Designing with
intention.

I am Gülcan, an architect based in İzmir, Türkiye. I work across architecture and interior design, with a particular interest in how the design process moves from an initial idea to a space that can be experienced.

My academic background and internship experience have allowed me to engage with concept development, technical drawing, architectural visualization and the practical side of design. I am especially interested in material choices, digital fabrication and the relationship between detail and the wider spatial experience.

I value a considered process: asking the right questions, exploring alternatives and bringing creative thinking together with technical clarity. My work continues to evolve through learning and collaboration across disciplines.
