Privacy & analytics

This portfolio uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate traffic and performance measurement and Statcounter to understand interest in the portfolio and improve the website.

Analytics data

Statcounter receives network information including IP addresses when a browser connects to its service. The project is configured not to store full IP addresses. Statcounter may record approximate location, pages viewed, referring website, visit time, browser and operating system. First-party visitor storage is disabled in the tracking code and the project-level cookie opt-out setting is enabled. The free account retains detailed records for the latest 500 page views; aggregate statistics may be retained longer. These services process data externally, potentially outside your country. See Statcounter's privacy information and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Your choice

Statcounter is optional and loads only when you allow analytics. You can change or withdraw your analytics permission at any time. The preference is stored locally in your browser. The site respects Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track for Statcounter. Declining prevents future Statcounter tracking; it does not erase previously collected records or disable aggregate Cloudflare Web Analytics.
