Privacy & analytics

This portfolio uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate traffic and performance measurement. Optional Statcounter analytics is loaded only when you allow it.

Optional analytics

Statcounter may record your IP address, approximate location, pages viewed, referring website, visit time, browser and operating system, First-party Statcounter storage is disabled in the site's tracking code; the separate project-level cookie setting must also be disabled to prevent third-party analytics cookies. The information is used to understand interest in the portfolio and improve the website. The free account retains detailed records for the latest 500 page views; aggregate statistics may be retained longer. Statcounter processes these data as an external service. See Statcounter's privacy information and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Your choice

You can decline optional analytics or change your choice using Analytics settings in the footer of the Home, About or Portfolio page. Your choice is saved in your browser. Declining prevents future Statcounter tracking on this site; it does not erase records already collected. You can remove existing tracking cookies using your browser's settings.

For privacy questions or requests concerning your data, use the contact form.
